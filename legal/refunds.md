## Subscriptions

- **First payment — 14-day guarantee.** If you are not happy with Pro within 14 days of your **first** subscription payment, email us and we will refund it in full, no questions asked. This applies once per account.

- **Duplicate or erroneous charges** are always refunded — contact us.

## Prepaid credits

- Credit top-ups are **refundable in full only if none of the balance has been used**.
- Once any part of a top-up has been used, the remainder is **non-refundable**, except where the law of your country requires otherwise.
- If we discontinue hosted models or the Service, we will refund any unused credit balance.

# Nutrition & Health Disclaimer

**DRAFT — requires attorney review before launch.** Version 2026-09-19.

Aboyeur produces meal plans, macro targets, shopping lists and nutrition estimates. Please read this before relying on any of it.

**Informational only.** Everything the Service produces is general information for planning purposes. It is not medical advice, medical nutrition therapy, or a substitute for the individual judgement of a licensed physician, registered dietitian or other qualified professional who knows your situation.

**Talk to a professional first.** Consult a physician before making significant dietary changes, starting a weight-loss or weight-gain programme, or changing how you eat if you have — or suspect you have — any medical condition.

**Numbers are estimates.** Calorie and macronutrient figures are calculated from public datasets (USDA FoodData Central) combined with automated interpretation of recipe text by an AI model. Portion sizes, cooking losses and ingredient substitutions all change the real values. Treat every number as approximate.

**Allergen information may be incomplete.** Aboyeur filters recipes using the allergies you enter and the ingredient lists it can read, but ingredient lists in older cookbooks are often incomplete, and cross-contamination is never modelled. If an allergy is life-threatening, verify every ingredient yourself.

**Not for these situations without professional supervision:**

- diagnosed medical conditions requiring dietary management (diabetes, kidney disease, cardiovascular disease, and others)
- eating-disorder treatment or recovery
- pregnancy and breastfeeding
- infants, children and adolescents
- condition-specific or therapeutic diets (renal, ketogenic for epilepsy, low-FODMAP for IBS, and similar)
- very-low-calorie diets

**Not for clinical use.** Aboyeur is not designed, validated or offered for use in healthcare settings, is not HIPAA-compliant, and must not be used to store or process protected health information on behalf of a covered entity.

**Practitioners.** If you use Aboyeur to plan for clients, you remain solely responsible for the advice you give them, for staying within your scope of practice, and for obtaining any consent needed to enter their information.

Stop and seek medical help if you experience adverse effects from any dietary change.
